About AVYAKT

The official Information Science & Engineering student club of Kalpataru Institute of Technology, Tiptur — affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi.

Our story

What is AVYAKT?

AVYAKT is a student-run community for everyone in the Department of Information Science & Engineering. Founded in 2026 by students who wanted more than the syllabus, it is a department-wide platform for learning, building and competing.

We believe the best engineers are made outside the classroom — by shipping projects, breaking things, learning from mentors and working in teams. AVYAKT exists to make that possible for every student, regardless of year or prior experience.

At a glance

  • Founded: 2026
  • Department: Information Science & Engineering
  • College: Kalpataru Institute of Technology, Tiptur
  • University: VTU Belagavi
Vision & mission

What we stand for

🔭

Vision

To build the most active student community in the department — where every member gains real skills, takes part in competitions and events, and grows into a leader.

🎯

Mission

To organise technical, cultural and sports events through the year, support student projects, and connect members with mentors, alumni and industry.

🌟

Goals

Grow participation across all years, host every kind of event, and help every member graduate with a strong portfolio.
