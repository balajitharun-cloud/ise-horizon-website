About ISE HORIZON

The official Information Science & Engineering student club of Kalpataru Institute of Technology, Tiptur — affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi.

Our story

What is ISE HORIZON?

ISE HORIZON is a student-run community for everyone in the Department of Information Science & Engineering. Founded in 2021 by a small group of students who wanted more than the syllabus, it has grown into a department-wide platform for learning, building and competing.

We believe the best engineers are made outside the classroom — by shipping projects, breaking things, learning from mentors and working in teams. HORIZON exists to make that possible for every student, regardless of year or prior experience.

At a glance

  • Founded: 2021
  • Department: Information Science & Engineering
  • College: Kalpataru Institute of Technology, Tiptur
  • University: VTU Belagavi
  • Members: 600+ active students
  • Events hosted: 40+
Vision & mission

What we stand for

Vision

🎯

Mission

To run consistent, high-quality learning experiences, nurture student projects, and connect students with mentors, alumni and industry.

🌟

Grow participation across all years, ship more open-source projects, and place every member with strong technical foundations.
